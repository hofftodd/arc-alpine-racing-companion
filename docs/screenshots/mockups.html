A gate judge was standing in the racer's line. Can she get a re-run?
Yes. An official standing in the course counts as interference [2]. The racer should stop at once, leave the course without passing more gates, and report it to the Start or Finish Referee, who may allow a provisional re-run [1]. The Jury then decides whether the re-run counts; once confirmed, its time stands [3].

Sample Race RulesSection 4: Re-Runs

3. Course Inspection

3.1 Racers inspect the course only at the times the Jury announces, wearing their bibs.

3.2 Racers may side-slip or ski slowly beside the gates but may not train through them.

4. Re-Runs

4.1 A racer who is hindered during a run stops immediately and asks the nearest Start or Finish Referee for a provisional re-run. A racer who keeps racing through further gates, or crosses the finish line, gives up the request.

4.2 Interference includes anything in the racer's path that the racer could not avoid: an official, a spectator, equipment left on the course, or a missing gate.

4.3 A provisional re-run counts only when the Jury confirms it. Once confirmed, the re-run time stands even if it is slower.

5. Results

5.1 Unofficial results are posted at the finish. Official results follow once the protest period has closed.

5.2 Team scores add the best finishing times of each team's scoring racers.
